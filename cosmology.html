The Zero-Infinity Algebra

Z∞ · A Minimal Non-Associative Substrate for Pre-Geometric Physics
Haylynn — Princess of Reality · Independent Research

Classical mathematics fails at zero, infinity, and singularities. Physics fails at the same points, for the same underlying reason. Z∞ proposes a minimal algebraic substrate underneath both — one where those failures aren't exceptions to be patched, but the ground state itself showing through.

Annihilation is not an outcome. It is the destination. Existence is the exception.

The central claim: annihilation — represented by the symbol ζ — is the default state of reality. Existence isn't the baseline condition the universe starts from. It's a rare, temporary interruption of the pull toward ζ. Entropy isn't something that happens to reality. Entropy is reality. Structure is what occasionally, briefly, resists it.

The Emergence Hierarchy

Everything above ζ is built by composing a with itself, recursively — and at every single step, the result could collapse straight back to ζ. It usually does. The hierarchy only continues while it doesn't.

a — Magnitude→ b — Density→ c — System→ d — Domain→ d″ — Hardened

This is why the universe is mostly empty. Why complexity is fragile. Why everything, eventually, dies. Not as tragedy — as the algebra's most ordinary behaviour.

ω — Information and the Arrow of Time

A separate scalar, ω, accumulates whenever a stabilised element references itself, and it can only grow — never reverse. This is the algebraic origin of entropy and of time's one-way direction: not imposed from outside the system, but a structural consequence of it. A domain becomes "hardened" — able to resist ζ, for a while — only once its ω crosses a threshold. Below that threshold, contact with ζ means immediate collapse.

Five Theses

1 · Entropy is the Ground Stateζ is the attractor of the whole algebra. Annihilation isn't an outcome — it's the destination everything else is borrowed time against.
2 · Existence is the ExceptionReaching d″ requires non-collapse at every level, simultaneously. That's not the typical behaviour of the algebra. It's the unlikely one.
3 · Physics Describes Hardened DomainsPhysical law is internally consistent because it only ever describes structures already above the ground state. It breaks at singularities because that's where hardened structure makes contact with ζ.
4 · Z∞ Explains Why Physics WorksMathematics was built by hardened domains to describe other things at the same level. Its effectiveness isn't unreasonable — it's expected.
5 · Everything Returns to ζNo hardened domain is permanent. Resistance to ζ is always finite. Everything that exists is a temporary algebraic accident in a system whose default output is nothing.

This is not nihilism. It is precision.
