Privacy

How this house treats information

This page describes the public site at haylynn.github.io/haylynn (and any domain that later replaces it). It is written for visitors in the UK and elsewhere. It is not legal advice.

What this site is

A public storytelling and art site for Haylynn, Princess of Reality — story, language, music, and related rooms. Some rooms are still forming.

What we collect

By default, as little as possible. The static and interactive pages do not require an account to walk the scroll.

  • Hosting logs. The host (currently GitHub Pages) may process standard technical data such as IP address, time, and requested path — under their own terms.
  • Local device storage. Some features (for example a remembered draw history, or a dismissed banner) may use your browser’s local storage or session storage. That data stays on your device unless you clear it.
  • Location (optional). The small weather control only runs if you allow location. If you refuse, it stays off.
  • Accounts (when enabled). If you create an account later, we will process the email and profile details you provide, on the auth provider named at sign-up, under that provider’s terms and our membership wording.
  • Payments (when enabled). Card payments are handled by the payment provider (for example Stripe, PayPal, or GoFundMe). We do not store full card numbers on this site.

Cookies and similar tech

Essential operation of the site may use storage required for the page to work. Non-essential analytics or marketing cookies are not enabled by default. If that changes, the cookie banner will offer a choice before non-essential cookies are set, and this page will be updated.

Third parties

Embedded players, maps, fonts, or external galleries (for example music or photography hosts) are governed by those services. Following a link to MOLI fundraising or another cause leaves this site’s control for that provider’s rules.

Your choices

You can refuse location, clear site data in your browser, and stop using the site at any time. Account deletion and payment history, when those systems are live, will follow the process described at Threshold and in the provider dashboards.

Contact

For privacy questions about this site, use the contact given on the organiser’s public profiles or the address published when accounts go live. Update this paragraph with a stable email before wide launch.
