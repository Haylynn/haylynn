Privacy

How this house treats information

This page describes the public site at haylynn.github.io/haylynn (and any domain that later replaces it). It is written for visitors in the UK and elsewhere. It is not legal advice.

What this site is

A public storytelling and art site for Haylynn, Princess of Reality — story, language, music, membership experiments, and related rooms. Some rooms are still forming.

Who is responsible

The site is operated by the person or organisation that publishes this project (the “operator”). Until a company or charity is formally named here, treat the contact method below as the route for privacy questions. This page will be updated when a formal controller name is fixed.

What we collect

You can walk the scroll without an account.

  • Hosting logs. The host (currently GitHub Pages) may process technical data such as IP address, time, and requested path — under their terms.
  • Local device storage. Features such as draw history, veil dismiss, Path progress offline, or simulated Zoo balances may use local or session storage on your device until you clear site data.
  • Location (optional). Weather only runs if you allow location. Refuse and it stays off. Forecast requests go to Open-Meteo when enabled.
  • Accounts. If you sign up, we process email, password (handled by the auth provider), and profile fields you choose (name, handle, bio, links, avatar). Authentication is provided by Supabase. Account-linked progress (for example Path unlocks) may be stored in that project’s database under access rules (RLS) set by the operator.
  • Payments (when enabled). Card payments go through the payment provider (for example Stripe, PayPal, or GoFundMe). We do not store full card numbers on this site.
  • Simulated house balances (Zoo / 0∞). Test or simulated balances are house points for Path and doors. They are not bank money. See Terms.

Cookies and similar tech

Essential storage may be required for the page to work. Non-essential analytics or marketing cookies are not enabled by default. If that changes, a choice will be offered before non-essential cookies are set, and this page will be updated.

Third parties

  • Supabase — accounts and related data when you sign in.
  • SoundCloud — embedded music player for Talu when Voice is used.
  • Google Fonts — typefaces.
  • Open-Meteo — optional weather.
  • esm.sh — delivery of certain front-end libraries.
  • External galleries, fundraising hosts, or photography sites (for example LYMP) under their own policies.

Your choices (UK and similar regimes)

You can refuse location, clear site data in your browser, and stop using the site. Where accounts exist, you may request access or deletion of account data via the contact below or tools in Threshold when offered. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO). Providers above have their own rights processes.

Children

The site is aimed at a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Do not create an account if you are under the minimum age required in your country for the services used (auth and payments may require 16+ or 18+ depending on provider rules).
